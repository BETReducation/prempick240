PP Cup

A side competition that includes FA Cup games and is arranged as a knockout tournament.

PP Cup Rules
  1. During a match week with FA Cup games instead of Premier League games, fixtures will include FA Cup games.
  2. The predictions still count towards the overall Rankings, and therefore also be ellible for the Praise Jackpot
  3. Additionally, the predictions will also contribute to the PP Cup knockout tournament.
  4. When Round 3 fixtures are known in the FA Cup, a random draw will be made for this PP Cup Tournament.
  5. Byes will be awarded for random players if the number of players doesn't match a knockout tournament bracket.
  6. Each round of the FA Cup will have fixtures for this competition (sometimes a Premier League game may be used to make up 6 fixtures if needed).
  7. At the end of the season, the PP Cup winner will win a certificate, and win a 5% share of any built-up praise in the kitty.
