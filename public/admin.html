The six fixtures

Ties need no fixture of their own — a tie's "score" is simply each player's results-correct count in the gameweek you assign to its round. A drawn tie shows "needs a replay"; add a replay gameweek and it resolves automatically.

Groups play a round-robin, one matchday per international gameweek — tag that gameweek's fixtures INTL on the Fixtures tab so they read as internationals. Each matchday's "goals" are the two players' results-correct counts in that gameweek.
